This app brings Swedish buses, trains, trams, the metro, road traffic and road ferries into Homey. Add any stop or station as a departure board with live delays, cancellations and train tracks, watch a road for accidents, queues and slippery conditions with a live traffic camera, or set up your daily commute once and see door-to-door options, the car route and the weather on your dashboard. Dashboard widgets show departure boards, your commute, a "time to leave" countdown, traffic cameras and road ferries, and Flow cards tell you when it is time to leave, when to start the engine heater, and when something goes wrong on the way.

You need two free keys, one from Trafiklab and one from Trafikverket; the app settings show step by step how to get them. Your keys and settings stay on your Homey, and addresses you search for in the settings are looked up with OpenStreetMap. Data from Trafiklab (CC BY 4.0), Trafikverket, SMHI (CC BY 4.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors. The app is not affiliated with them or with any transport operator.
