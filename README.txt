Never miss your bus again. See when to leave home, with live delays and cancellations for buses, trains, trams and the metro anywhere in Sweden, and keep an eye on the roads you drive: accidents, slippery roads and live traffic cameras. Put it on your dashboard, or let Homey tell you when it is time to go and when something goes wrong on the way.

You need two free keys, one from Trafiklab and one from Trafikverket; the app settings show step by step how to get them. Your keys and settings stay on your Homey, and addresses you search for are looked up with OpenStreetMap. Data from Trafiklab (CC BY 4.0), Trafikverket, SMHI (CC BY 4.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors. The app is not affiliated with them or with any transport operator.
